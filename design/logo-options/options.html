Satellite mark options (blue), beside Orbit

Orbit (for reference)
The existing family mark.
1 Sputnik
The first satellite: a sphere trailing antennae.
2 Solar panels
The satellite everyone draws: body and two panel wings.
3 Tilted orbit
Orbit's ring tipped into 3D, a satellite riding the front.
4 Horizon
The planet's edge, and a satellite climbing over it.
5 Page in orbit
An article with a satellite circling it.
6 Dish
Receiving: a dish catching what the satellites send.
7 S orbit
Two half-orbits make an S, with a satellite at each end.
8 Crescent
A moon and the small satellite sharing its sky.
9 Ringed planet
A planet with a tight ring passing in front.
10 Page satellite
A satellite whose body is a page of text.
